Content is reviewed before it becomes public, and the review is a conversation
rather than a verdict shouted from behind a door.

## The queue

Submitted projects wait in a queue, oldest first, so the person who has waited
longest is not at the bottom of the page.

## Decisions

A moderator publishes, rejects or removes. A rejection or a removal **must** come
with a message — a decision you cannot read is one you cannot act on.

## Appealing

Reply in the project's thread. If the project was rejected, replying puts it back
in the queue automatically, because an appeal that changes nothing is a message
nobody is scheduled to read.

## What gets refused

Anything that is not yours to publish, malware, content that misrepresents what
it does, spam, and name squatting. A flagged file blocks publishing until it has
been looked at.
