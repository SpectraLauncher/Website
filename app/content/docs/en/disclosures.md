A project can state things about itself that a reader deserves to know before
downloading it.

- **Made with AI**, and whether that was code, assets, text or functionality
- **Contains advertisements**
- **May trigger epilepsy** — flashing, strobing
- **Interacts with your system** beyond the game
- **Collects telemetry**, and whether that is opt-in or on by default
- **Derivative work**, and from what
- **Has paid features**

Each can carry a note.

## Why a moderator can pin one

If moderation establishes that a project does something — collects telemetry, say
— they can pin that declaration so it cannot quietly be unticked. Three states:
the author may change it, the author may not remove it, or it is fixed entirely.

Declarations show on the project page, so nobody has to read a description
looking for them.
