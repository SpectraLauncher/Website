An organization owns projects on behalf of a group, so a project does not
depend on one person's account.

## Roles

**Owner**, **admin** and **member**. Roles set the default rights; the rights
themselves are individual.

## Permissions

Each member has their own set: edit the organization, invite people, remove
people, edit members, add projects, remove projects, delete the organization.

Two rules run through all of it:

- You cannot hand out a permission you do not hold yourself
- You cannot act on somebody who stands level with you or above you

An owner always holds everything, whatever the boxes say, so an organization can
never lock itself out. An organization always has at least one owner: the last
one cannot be demoted, removed, or leave.

## Projects

A project owned by an organization is worked on by its members. Being in the
organization gets you the working set — upload versions, edit details, edit the
description, see analytics. Deleting, member management and payouts have to be
granted per project.
