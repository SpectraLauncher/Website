Every listing has the same shape: filters on the left, results on the right.

## Filters

- **Game version** — the version you are playing
- **Loader** — Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Quilt, or a server platform
- **Category** — what kind of thing it is
- **Environment** — client, server or both
- **Licence**

Filters combine. Picking two game versions means "either of these", picking a
game version and a loader means "both".

## Sorting

By downloads, by follows, by when it was updated, by when it was published, or by
relevance when you have typed a search.

## Layout

Results show as a grid or as rows. The choice sticks between visits.
