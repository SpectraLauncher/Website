A project can be sold rather than given away. Payments run through Stripe.

## Setting up

Under **Seller** you connect a Stripe account. Selling needs one that has
finished Stripe's onboarding — that is where identity and bank details live, not
here.

An organization can have its own seller account, separate from yours.

## Commission

We take a share of each sale:

| Who is selling | Commission |
| --- | --- |
| An account | 1% |
| An organization | 1% |
| A verified organization | 0.5% |
| A partner | 0.5% |

Currencies are EUR and USD. The commission is taken at the moment of the charge,
so what reaches you is already net.

## Becoming a partner or a verified organization

Both go through an application and a moderator's decision. Apply under
**Verification**.

## What buyers get

A purchase is permanent and tied to the account that made it. It appears in the
buyer's **Library**, and downloads stay available there.
