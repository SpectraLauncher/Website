Your account is how the site knows what is yours: your projects, your
collections, the things you follow, and anything you have bought.

## Signing in

You can sign in with an e-mail address and password, with a linked account
(Discord, Google, GitHub, Microsoft), or with a **passkey** — your fingerprint,
your face, or a security key, with no password to remember or leak.

Add a passkey under **Settings → Security**.

## Two-factor authentication

Under **Settings → Security** you can turn on a one-time code from an
authenticator app. Save the backup codes somewhere that is not your phone.

## Sessions

**Settings → Sessions** lists every device signed in to your account, with what
it is and when it was last used. Anything you do not recognise can be signed out
from here.

## Your profile

**Settings → Profile** holds your display name, username, avatar, a short bio and
your links. All of it shows on your public page at `/u/your-username`.

## Notifications

**Settings → Notifications** decides how each kind of notification reaches you.
In-app is always on — the bell is the record of what happened — and e-mail is
per group.

## Blocking

**Settings → Blocked** hides someone's comments from you and stops them reaching
you. They are never told.

## Deleting your account

**Settings → Privacy** has account deletion, with a preview of what goes with it.
It refuses while somebody else would be left holding a broken thing: an
organization you solely own, or completed sales that have to stay attributable.
