Money is tracked as a ledger, not as a balance somebody keeps up to date.

## Why a ledger

Every event is a row: a sale, the commission on it, a refund, a payout, a
correction. A balance is the sum of those rows, computed every time it is asked
for.

That means any figure can be explained by pointing at the rows that made it,
which matters the first time somebody disagrees about a number.

## Reading it

**Revenue** shows your balance per currency, what was earned, what commission was
taken, and what has already been paid out — plus the full list of entries.

## Requesting a payout

Ask for one from the same page. There is a minimum, because below it the transfer
costs more than it moves. A payout appears as a negative entry immediately, so
the same money cannot be requested twice.

If a payout fails or is cancelled, the money comes back as its own entry rather
than by deleting the failed one. The ledger keeps showing what was attempted.
