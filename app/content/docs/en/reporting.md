If something is wrong, report it. The flag icon is on project pages and on
individual comments.

## What to say

Pick a reason — spam, copyright, inappropriate content, malicious file, name
squatting, or something else — and be specific. Which file, which line, which
claim. A moderator reads every report, and a vague one is slower to act on.

## One report per thing

You can have one open report per item. That is deliberate: it stops a queue being
flooded, and it means ten rows are ten people rather than one person ten times.

## What happens next

Your report appears under **My reports**, with its status and a conversation. A
moderator can ask you a question there, and you will hear when a decision is
made.

A report that vanishes silently teaches people not to bother reporting, so it
does not.
